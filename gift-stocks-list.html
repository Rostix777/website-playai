Which stock will I get?

Since the stock is randomly selected, the gift can be any of the following securities. Please note that higher-value securities are allocated with a lower probability than lower-value securities.

The gift stock is selected at random from the list above via a weighted random algorithm. Participants cannot choose the issuer. The probability of receiving a particular stock is inversely proportional to its market value.

Prices shown are indicative and may differ at the time of allocation. All securities are traded on US exchanges.
